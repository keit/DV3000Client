DV3K Client

A Linux desktop client for D-Star reflectors and DMR networks, using a ThumbDV (AMBE-3000) USB dongle for the voice codec.

  • D-Star via DExtra: XLX and XRF reflectors, searchable from the live XLX directory.
  • DMR on BrandMeister (Open DMR Terminal) and TGIF (Homebrew), with talkgroup search and favourites.
  • Push-to-talk with your PC's microphone and speaker, level meters, and a Last Heard list.
  • The ThumbDV can be plugged into your PC, or on a Raspberry Pi running AMBEServer.

There's no software vocoder: you need a ThumbDV to transmit or hear anything.
